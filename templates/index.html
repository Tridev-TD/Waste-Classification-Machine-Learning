    About

    Reclaim uses MobileNetV2, a lightweight image-recognition network pretrained on ImageNet. Through transfer learning, its final layers are retrained on a public garbage-classification dataset (TrashNet-style: cardboard, glass, metal, paper, plastic, trash). It is a college micro project, so results are indicative only.
